## Rule #0 — Audit before you build anything
Many of the systems and features described in this plan may ALREADY exist in the codebase in some form, even if partial, buggy, or under a different name. Before implementing anything from this document:
1. **Search the existing codebase first** for a system that already covers what you're about to build.
2. If it exists but is incomplete/broken: **fix, optimize, and stabilize it** — do not rewrite it from scratch or replace it with a parallel new implementation.
3. If it exists and mostly works: leave the working parts alone, extend only what's missing, and don't "clean up" working code just for style.
4. Only build something genuinely new from zero if no equivalent exists at all.
5. The goal is a stable, polished, working game — not a rebuild. Prefer small, safe, well-tested changes over large rewrites at every step.

## Target Identity
A dark, cyberpunk-noir crime RPG where the car is the player's core identity object (not just the character sheet), crime is the primary income loop, hacking is a genuinely different interaction pattern from the rest of the game, and a real-life simulation layer (careers, housing, relationships, health) gives players a legal alternative path. Visual tone: near-black backgrounds, an electric cyan or violet accent (avoid red/gray — that's the old Torn-style palette), amber/orange accents on car-related screens to separate "mechanical" from "digital" screens, subtle glitch/scanline texture only on cyber-layer screens.

## Full System List To Implement/Reskin — with concrete mechanics

### 1. Core resource & vitals (reskin existing)
- HP -> Health, Energy -> Energy, Nerve -> **Heat**, add new **Stress** bar
- Regen: lazy-computed, `current = min(max, current + floor(minutes_elapsed / regen_interval))`, updated on every read/spend
  - Energy: +1 per 10 min, base max 100
  - Heat: does NOT regen like the others — it **rises** with crime activity (`heat += run_difficulty_tier * 4`) and **decays** passively at -1 per 5 min when the player is not actively running crimes
  - Stress: rises on combat losses (+10), failed runs (+5), long work shifts without rest (+2/hr); decays via therapy items, sleep, or vacations
- Stress effects: above 70/100, apply a -10% success-chance penalty to all Run and Race actions until reduced — this makes stress a real mechanic, not flavor text
- Max values are derived stats: `max_energy = 100 + (level * 2) + perk_bonuses`, recalculated on read, never stored directly

### 1b. Gym — Player Stat Training (new, does not exist yet — audit first per Rule #0 in case a partial version exists)
The game currently has Strength/Charisma/Intelligence/Street Cred stats but no dedicated training system for them (unlike Torn's gym). Add a Gym exactly the way Torn's works, reskinned to fit this game's identity:
- **Gym locations**: 3-5 gym tiers (e.g. "Corner Boxing Gym" -> "Underground Fight Club" -> "Elite Performance Center"), each with a `cost_per_use`, an `energy_cost_per_train`, and per-stat "gym gain multipliers" (some gyms are better for Strength, others for Intelligence, etc.) — this gives players a reason to travel/unlock better gyms as they progress, exactly like Torn's gym list.
- **Training action**: spend Energy (not a separate resource) to train one stat at a time in a chosen gym:
  `stat_gain = (energy_spent * gym_multiplier[stat] * happy_or_stress_modifier) / (1 + current_stat_value / diminishing_constant)^1.5`
  — same diminishing-curve shape already used for car tuning in Section 3, so the two systems feel consistent
- **The four core stats and what they should affect** (make sure each stat has a real downstream use, not just a number to grind):
  - **Strength**: combat damage output (PvP/PvE), success chance on Muscle-role heist checks (Section 2 co-op heists), carrying capacity for stolen goods
  - **Street Cred (renamed from Torn's "Speed")**: Run success chance modifier, unlocks higher-tier Runs/Black Market access, reduces "sting risk" slightly at high values
  - **Charisma**: better prices in Market negotiations, higher fame/follower growth rate (Section 8), improves Lawyer/Bribe outcomes in the Court system (Section 5)
  - **Intelligence**: Net/hacking minigame difficulty reduction, Business management efficiency (higher business income cap), Informant/Trace success chance (Section 2)
- **Stress/Happy interaction**: exactly like Torn's happy-boosts-gains mechanic, a `mood` or `morale` stat (fed by Stress being low, wins, rest) should apply a temporary multiplier to gym gains — this ties the new Stress bar (Section 1) into the gym loop instead of leaving it isolated
- **Gym item examples** (consumables/boosts, sold via Market for DC, some rare versions for NC per Section 13):
  - **Energy Refill** (small/medium/large) — instantly restores a fixed amount of Energy for one extra training session; small/medium DC-priced, an oversized "Full Refill" version could be NC-priced as a convenience item (never required)
  - **Protein Shake / Nootropic Stack** — temporary (e.g. 1 hour) flat % boost to gains for one specific stat (Strength shake, Intelligence nootropic, etc.) — DC-priced, common consumable
  - **Steroid-analog item** (e.g. "Synth-Boost") — large temporary gain boost but adds a Stress/Heat penalty on use, or has a cooldown and a small chance of a "bad batch" side-effect (temporary stat debuff) — a genuine risk/reward item, not a pure upgrade, consistent with the plan's "no free power" principle
  - **Gym Pass** — removes `cost_per_use` for a set number of sessions or a time window (e.g. 24h unlimited gym) — good NC or Merit candidate since it's pure convenience, not raw power
  - **Trainer NPC hire** — a City-side (Section 5) hireable NPC with a daily upkeep fee that passively boosts one stat's gain rate while active, without requiring active play — ties Gym into the City system rather than leaving it fully isolated
- **Anti-abuse note**: apply the same rate-limiting principle from Section 11 to training actions — cap trains-per-hour server-side independent of Energy cost, so a scripted bot can't out-train legitimate players even if it has infinite Energy from other exploits

### 2. Street (crime system, extend existing crimes)
- Reskin "crimes" as **Runs**. Keep the tiered list, but restructure tiers around car-crime + cyber-crime + street-crime archetypes instead of generic Torn crimes:
  - Tier 1 (low heat, low mastery required): Joyriding, Parking-meter scams, Small-time fencing
  - Tier 2: Chop-shop stripping, Street tax collection, Pickpocket-by-distraction
  - Tier 3: Armed carjacking, Warehouse smuggling run, Illegal street race betting fraud
  - Tier 4 (high heat, requires crew backup optional): Bank truck heist, Data-center break-in, Cartel supply run
  - Tier 5 (co-op only): The Big Heist — requires 3-5 crew members in distinct roles
- Success formula (server-side only):
  `success_chance = base_chance[tier] + (mastery_score * 0.4) + (relevant_stat / 10) - (current_stress * 0.1)`
  clamp to [5%, 95%] so there's always some risk and some hope
- Mastery: `action_mastery` row per `(player_id, run_id)`; each attempt (success OR fail) adds `+1` to mastery up to a soft cap of 100, with diminishing weight above 50 (`gain = 1 / (1 + mastery/50)`)
- Failure severity buckets, rolled after a failed success check:
  - 60% minor fail: no heat, no cash, wasted heat/energy only
  - 30% bad fail: +heat, small fine deducted from wallet
  - 10% severe fail: jail time (real-time timer, e.g. 10-60 min scaled to tier) or hospitalization if it was a combat-adjacent run
- **Heat & Pursuit**: once `heat >= 80`, the next Run attempt (success or fail) triggers a **Pursuit event** — a real-time reaction minigame (press-to-brake/turn sequence, ~10-15 sec) with 3 outcomes: Bribe (pay cash, heat resets to 40), Outrun (skill-check using car Speed + Handling, heat resets to 20 on success), Busted (jail, heat resets to 0)
- **Jail & Bail**: jailed players show up in a public jail list; any player can pay a bail formula (`bail_cost = base_fine * remaining_time_minutes * 1.5`) to free them (small reputation reward for the payer); jailed players can attempt one **Escape** roll per jail stay (`escape_chance = 20% + dexterity_stat/20`, failure adds time)
- **Black market**: separate item feed only visible after first successful Tier-2+ Run; goods here are untraceable (no purchase log tied to public profile) but carry a flat "sting risk" per purchase (`sting_chance = 3%`, resulting in item confiscation + heat spike)
- **Money laundering**: `clean_cash = dirty_cash * (1 - business_launder_rate)`, where `launder_rate` starts at 30% loss and improves as the owned business levels up (down to 10% loss at max level) — processed through the City business system, with a processing time delay (not instant) to make it feel like a real operation
- **Co-op heists**: require a crew party of 3-5 with role tags (Hacker, Driver, Muscle, Lookout); each role runs its own mini-check (Hacker = Net puzzle, Driver = Garage timing minigame, Muscle = combat stat check, Lookout = passive heat-suppression roll); overall heist succeeds only if a weighted majority of role checks pass; payout splits automatically by role weight
- **Informants**: a player can file an anonymous tip on another player's last known Run (costs a small fee, has a cooldown); tipped player's heat spikes instantly; if discovered (via a Net "trace" action), the informant's identity is revealed and takes a reputation/heat hit themselves — creates real risk to snitching
- **Corruption/bribes**: a standing "bribe an official" action costs scaling cash and temporarily (per-use) reduces sentence length by 50% or removes 30 heat instantly; overuse should raise price via a per-day cooldown-scaled cost, not availability caps

### 3. Garage (new car-centric layer, replaces/extends "gym")
- Car object fields: `power, handling, grip, nitro, durability, paint_id, decal_ids[], stance, plate_text, vin, build_history[]`
- **Tuning (replaces gym training)**: spend Energy + cash + optionally parts on one stat at a time
  `gain = (energy_spent * base_tune_rate * part_quality_multiplier) / (1 + current_stat_value/25)^1.5`
  — identical diminishing-curve shape to Torn's STR/DEF/SPD/DEX training, just re-labeled
- Part types (examples): Turbo Kit (+Power), Sport Suspension (+Handling), Racing Slicks (+Grip), NOS Canister (+Nitro capacity), each with Common/Rare/Legendary quality tiers multiplying the tune rate (1x/1.5x/2.2x)
- **Races**: three modes —
  - Drag: straight-line, resolved by `finish_time = base_time - (power*0.02) - (nitro_used*0.01) + random_jitter`, plus a player-timed "perfect launch" button press (early = false start penalty, on-time = -0.3s bonus)
  - Circuit: multi-lap, resolved each lap by `lap_time = base - handling*0.015 - grip*0.01`, plus a live cornering minigame (hold a direction key through a shrinking window) affecting a per-lap time modifier
  - Drift: scored not timed — `score = angle_held * duration * (grip*0.3 + style_multiplier)`, players manage a live drift-angle meter, staying in the "sweet zone" too long risks a spin-out (score reset for that pass)
- **Pink-slip races**: a race flagged at creation as pink-slip; loser's car (chosen car, not always their only one) transfers ownership to winner atomically post-race, with a confirmation step before the race starts so it's never accidental
- **Chop shop**: convert an owned or stolen car into 3-6 random parts (weighted by car's stat levels), consumes the car permanently; ties into crime system as a Run outcome option ("strip this stolen car instead of selling it whole")
- **Impound/Body Shop** (reskinned hospital): a car that loses badly in combat/pursuit/street racing enters `impounded_until: timestamp`; player can pay to rush-release (cash scaling with remaining time) or wait it out; while impounded, that car can't be used for Runs or Races

### 4. Net (new cyber layer)
- **Hacking minigame**: a timed pattern-matching or Simon-Says-style sequence UI (client renders the puzzle, but the target sequence and pass/fail threshold are generated and verified server-side to prevent client tampering); difficulty (sequence length/speed) scales with target's `firewall_level`
- **Crypto currency**: a NEW fourth ledger, `SIM` coin — a speculative, volatile asset distinct from DC/NC/Merit, earned/traded only through Net activity; price recalculated hourly server-side using a formula like `price = base_price * (1 + (net_buy_volume - net_sell_volume) / liquidity_constant)`, capped per-hour swing (e.g. ±15%) to avoid runaway spirals; visible price history chart. SIM is NOT a substitute for NC — it's an in-world speculative currency players can win or lose, not a store-bought one.
- **Dark web marketplace**: listings tagged with a pseudonymous seller handle (not real username) generated per-listing; separate 1-5 star buyer/seller rating tied to the pseudonym, not the main profile
- **Drone/surveillance**: deploy a drone (costs Energy or a charge-limited item) on a target to reveal hidden info before acting — car stats before a race challenge, crew treasury balance before a heist, firewall level before a hack attempt; drone has a discovery chance (`spotted_chance = 15% - stealth_upgrades`) that alerts the target
- **Cybersecurity investment**: `firewall_level` stat purchasable with DC/SIM, directly raises the difficulty of the hacking minigame targeted at this player and reduces `sting_chance`/`spotted_chance` against them
- **Identity theft**: a successful high-difficulty hack on a player grants a time-limited (e.g. 10 min) "borrowed identity" token letting the attacker perform ONE restricted action as the victim (e.g. list a black-market item under their name); always logged and discoverable by the victim afterward via an activity log entry
- **AI handler NPC**: a dialogue-flavored quest dispenser; mechanically it's just a rotating job-offer table (pulls from the Run/Race/Hack pools with a bonus modifier) refreshed every few hours — narrative wrapper over existing systems, not a new resolution engine

### 5. City (new real-life simulation layer)
- **Careers**: job ladder table `(job_id, tier, salary_per_shift, stat_gain, promotion_requirement)`; working a shift consumes Energy and time, pays out salary directly to wallet (always clean cash, unlike Runs)
- **Housing**: property tiers with `storage_capacity, hideout_security_bonus (reduces chance of being raided while offline), rent_per_day`; owning removes rent but costs a large upfront price
- **Relationships**: partner status grants a shared perk (e.g. +5% passive income or shared storage); breakup has a one-time cash/reputation cost
- **Businesses**: `(business_id, level, daily_income, launder_capacity_per_day, upgrade_cost)`; income accrues lazily like the resource bars (compute elapsed-time earnings on visit) up to a max holding cap that requires the player to collect it
- **Banking**: savings interest `balance * 0.5%/day` (paid lazily on read), loans with a fixed schedule and an escalating penalty/collections event (a debt collector combat encounter) on missed payments
- **Insurance**: flat daily premium; payout formula on a covered loss event = `min(claim_value, policy_cap) * payout_rate`; insurance fraud = deliberately triggering a "theft" of your own insured car via a Run, flagged with a hidden fraud-detection roll that can backfire (denied claim + heat spike) if caught
- **Taxes**: a flat % skimmed automatically from all Career/Business income (not Run income, since that's already "dirty"); unpaid tax above a threshold triggers an audit event
- **Court system**: on arrest, a trial screen offers Plead Guilty (fixed reduced sentence) vs Fight It (a stat/lawyer-check with a chance of full dismissal but longer sentence on failure); lawyers are hireable NPCs/players that improve the fight-it odds
- **Aging/legacy**: optional — a "Retire" action available after reaching a level threshold, converts a % of net worth + one chosen perk into a permanent account-level bonus applied to the player's next character

### 6. Crew (extend existing factions)
- Treasury ledger: every deposit/withdrawal is a row `(crew_id, actor_id, amount, reason, timestamp)`, exposed as an auditable feed to permitted ranks — not just a balance number
- **Turf races**: each contested district has a `controlling_crew_id` and a weekly race-series score; crews schedule races in that district, race results add points to the crew's control score, highest score at week's end takes/keeps control and its passive income bonus
- **Alliances & betrayal**: a formal treaty object between two crews with agreed terms (e.g. shared turf, no-attack pact); breaking it triggers an automatic one-time payout to the betrayer and a reputation penalty logged publicly in the news feed

### 7. Market (extend existing marketplace/auction/trade)
- **Storefronts**: personal listing pages per player, browsable by crew/city/global filters
- **Order Book**: standard best-bid/best-ask matching on identical item+quality combos, instant execution
- **Dark Market**: only reachable via Net, pseudonymous as described above
- Auction House and Trade keep their current mechanics, just re-organized under this tab structure

### 8. You (extend profile/inventory/skills/achievements)
- **Cybernetic implants**: each implant gives a flat/percentage stat boost but adds a fixed amount to a "Humanity Loss" counter; crossing thresholds (e.g. 3, 6, 9 implants) permanently raises baseline Stress regen or unlocks dark-flavor perks — a real trade-off curve, not a pure buff
- **Alter ego**: a second named identity slot; actions taken as the alter ego post to a separate public activity log and don't affect the main identity's reputation/heat, but sharing a wallet means cash is NOT separated — only reputation/heat exposure is
- **Fame/influencer system**: `followers` grows from visible wins (races, heists reported in news); above follower thresholds, unlock passive "sponsorship income" paid daily, but also increase chance of being featured in the Tabloid feed (exposing your last known Run/Race to everyone, raising the odds you get targeted)
- **Tabloid/paparazzi feed**: a server-wide feed auto-populated from big public events (large heists, pink-slip losses, top ladder finishes) — pure derived content from existing event logs, no separate authoring needed

### 9. World (extend map/districts)
- District modifiers table: `(district_id, crime_payout_modifier, race_type_bonus, avg_item_price_modifier)` — read at action-time to adjust base formulas
- **Weather/time-of-day**: a global `world_state` row updated on a timer (e.g. every real hour) cycling `day/night` and `clear/rain`; night = `+15% Run payout, +10% heat gain`; rain = `-10% grip` applied as a race-time modifier
- **Seasons & ladder**: a `season_id` with start/end timestamps; ladder points earned from race/heist wins, tier cutoffs (Street/Underground/Circuit/Legend) recalculated nightly; season-end grants cosmetic-only rewards, then resets points (not levels/items)
- **World events**: scheduled or randomly-triggered rows (`event_type, starts_at, ends_at, effect_modifiers`) that temporarily change global formulas (e.g. "Crackdown Weekend": all heat gain +50%; "Market Crash": SIM price -30% instantly)
- **Companions**: hireable NPC rows with their own small stat sheet and upkeep cost (daily fee); a Driver companion adds a flat bonus to Race timing-window forgiveness, a Hacking Bot auto-completes low-tier Net puzzles at reduced reward, a Guard Dog reduces the chance of being successfully raided while offline

## Engineering Principles (apply to every new system)
- **Server-authoritative resolution only** — client never computes outcomes, only submits an attempt; server resolves in one atomic transaction
- **Lazy-computed resource regen** — no background cron for bars/timers; compute elapsed time on read/spend
- **Config-driven balancing** — costs, rewards, curves, and unlock thresholds live in data tables, not hardcoded, so they can be rebalanced without a redeploy
- **Full event/transaction logging** — every currency or resource change gets a ledger row, not just a balance update (required for treasury audits, dispute resolution, and exploit detection)
- **Diminishing returns on all progression curves** — no linear stat gain, to prevent power creep
- **One system at a time** — implement, wire to real data, verify it works end-to-end before starting the next; do not scaffold everything as stubs simultaneously

## What NOT to do
- Do not keep Torn's literal color palette (red/gray) — this game must look and feel visually distinct
- Do not resolve any crime/race/hack/combat outcome purely client-side
- Do not add these systems as flat/isolated features — each should hook into at least one other system (e.g. Heat affects both Street and Net; laundering connects Street and City; turf races connect Crew and Garage)
- Do not wait for owner approval on individual item/mission content additions within the systems above — only escalate for decisions outside this document's scope

## 10. Onboarding & Retention (new — required for launch, not optional polish)
- **Guided first-hour flow**: new accounts unlock tabs progressively — Street first (do 2-3 Tier-1 Runs), then Garage (get your first car), then City (get a job), then Net and Crew unlock after that. Don't expose all 9 tabs to a brand-new player on first login; gate the nav itself by an `onboarding_stage` field that advances on milestone actions.
- **Level-tier attack protection (already exists, audit + harden it per Rule #0)**: the game already restricts PvP attacks by level bracket (e.g. levels 11-20 cannot attack levels 1-10, and so on up the ladder). Do not rebuild this — locate the existing bracket-check logic and:
  - Verify the check is fully server-authoritative on every attack/target action (target list, attack submission, AND attack resolution) — a client-side-only or single-endpoint check can be bypassed by calling the attack API directly.
  - Confirm brackets can't be gamed by temporarily dropping level (if de-leveling is possible anywhere) or by using an alt account in a lower bracket to scout/harass a target's crew while the main account stays "protected" — cross-reference with the multi-accounting detection in Section 11.
  - Make sure the bracket boundaries and the "N brackets above/below" rule are config-driven (per Engineering Principles), not hardcoded, so they can be retuned without a redeploy if a bracket turns out to be exploited or too restrictive.
  - Apply the same bracket logic consistently to any new PvP-adjacent system introduced in this plan (Pursuit events, Pink-slip races, Informant tip-offs, Identity theft hacks, Turf races) — don't let a new system accidentally bypass protection that PvP combat already enforces.
  - Log every blocked out-of-bracket attack attempt (who tried, on whom) — a spike in blocked attempts against a specific player is a useful signal for harassment/targeting behavior even when the system successfully blocks it.
- **Explicit offline-progress rules**: define per-system what keeps running while offline vs. not —
  - Keeps running offline: business income accrual (up to a cap), car repair/impound timers, jail timers, banking interest
  - Blocked while offline: Runs, Races, Hacks, Pursuit events (nothing that requires an active decision)
  - This must be a documented, deliberate table (`system -> offline_behavior`), not left to fall out of the lazy-regen pattern by accident.

## 11. Anti-Abuse & Fairness (new — required before any real economy goes live)
- **Multi-accounting detection**: flag same-device/same-IP account clusters; apply reduced trade limits or disabled crew-treasury access between flagged accounts to prevent one person self-feeding resources via trades/laundering.
- **Rate limiting on economy-affecting actions**: server-side cooldown + max-attempts-per-hour enforcement on Runs, Hacks, and trades — independent of the resource cost — to block scripted/bot farming that would break resource scarcity.
- **Trade sanity checks**: flag trades with wildly lopsided value (e.g. rare car for near-nothing) for review — the most common laundering vector for multi-accounts.
- **Real-money trading (RMT) policy**: decide and document whether out-of-game sales of accounts/items/currency are bannable; enforce via ToS, not just informal policy.

## 12. Communication & Moderation Tooling (new — required at launch, chat already exists)
- **Report / mute / block** on global, district, and DM chat — must ship alongside chat, not after.
- **Admin economy dashboard**: internal view of total currency in circulation (DC, NC, Merit, SIM separately), average Heat/Stress levels server-wide, top earners, and SIM price history — so balancing decisions are based on real telemetry, not guesswork. Builds on the config-driven balancing tables already required in the Engineering Principles.

## 13. Monetization — Freemium (already live, extend it)
The game already runs a 3-currency economy — do not invent new top-level currencies, extend this one:
- **DC** — normal in-game currency, earned through gameplay (Runs, jobs, races, trades). This is the "dirty cash" / "clean cash" currency referenced throughout Sections 2-9 above.
- **NC** — premium currency, purchased with real money. Since there's no Stripe/payment processor wired up yet, NC purchases are currently handled manually through a Telegram bot/flow, not an in-app checkout. Keep this purchase path in mind: any new NC-priced item needs to work with a manually-fulfilled purchase flow (an admin/bot credits NC to an account), not assume an automated storefront checkout exists yet. If/when a real payment processor is added later, that's a scope change requiring owner sign-off, not something to build unprompted.
- **Merit (M)** — earned exclusively from achievements, not purchasable. Used as a separate reward currency for milestone-gated items (keep Merit-only shop items as achievement flexes, not power).
- **SIM** (new, Section 4) — a fourth, separate volatile in-world currency tied only to the Net/crypto system, earned or lost through gameplay, never purchasable with real money. Do not let SIM function as a backdoor way to buy NC-equivalent power.

All core gameplay systems above (Street, Garage, Net, City, Crew, Market, World) must be fully playable using only DC and Merit — NC must remain optional.

### What should be NC-priced (new features introduced in this plan)
- Garage: paint jobs, decal packs, plate styles/vanity plates, stance presets, alter-ego cosmetic skins (Section 8)
- Profile: frames (already exists), fame/tabloid "privacy shield" (temporarily hide from Tabloid feed — convenience, not power)
- Time-skips: instant Impound/Body Shop release, instant jail bail-reduction (partial only — full bail-skip would undercut the Jail & Bail social mechanic, so cap it), reduced business laundering processing delay
- Season pass (new): a paid track alongside a free track, purchased with NC; paid rewards are cosmetics + minor convenience (extra storage slot, small XP boost) — never a direct stat/power ceiling free players can't reach through play
- Slot expansions: extra alter-ego slot, extra business slot, extra garage storage slot — convenience/capacity, not raw power
- Companion cosmetic skins (Section 9) — NC; companion stat upgrades themselves should stay DC-purchasable so they're not pay-gated

### Explicitly NOT for sale with NC
Raw power stats, guaranteed Run/Hack/Race success, exclusive high-tier cars only obtainable via NC, cybernetic implants that give power without the Humanity Loss trade-off — anything that would make NC a pay-to-win lever breaks the freemium trust model and should be flagged back to the owner before implementing.

### Development status note
The game is still under active development and some existing features are incomplete. Before adding new NC-priced items to any given system (e.g. Garage cosmetics), the build agent should first check whether that system's core mechanic is actually finished — don't monetize a feature that doesn't fully work yet.

## Build Order (dependency-aware rollout sequence)
1. **Core resources & vitals** (Section 1) — everything else depends on Energy/Heat/Stress existing
1b. **Gym / player stat training** (Section 1b) — ship right after core vitals since Strength/Charisma/Intelligence/Street Cred feed directly into Street, Net, City, and Market success formulas below
2. **Street / Runs** (Section 2) — primary income loop, needed before economy systems make sense
3. **Garage / cars** (Section 3) — car ownership needed before Races, Chop Shop, or Pink-slips can work
4. **Market restructure** (Section 7) — needed before Net's Dark Market or City's businesses have anywhere to sell into
5. **City** (Section 5) — legal income + laundering destination for Street's dirty cash
6. **Net** (Section 4) — depends on Market (dark listings) and City (business firewall investment) existing first
7. **Crew** (Section 6) — turf races depend on Garage races existing; treasury ledger can ship earlier if needed standalone

## 15. Economy Balance Model (starting point — validate against real telemetry once live)
Before tuning exact numbers, model income vs. sinks per tier so the curves in Sections 1-9 don't need a painful post-launch correction:

- **Income per active hour, by tier** (rough target shape — replace with real numbers once formulas are finalized):
  - Tier 1 (new player): ~50-150 DC/hr from Runs + jobs combined
  - Tier 2-3 (mid game): ~300-800 DC/hr, increasingly gated behind Heat/Stress management
  - Tier 4-5 (late/co-op game): ~1000+ DC/hr but requires crew coordination and carries real risk (jail, car loss)
- **Sinks that should scale to match**: car tuning costs, business upgrade costs, bail costs, implant costs — each tier's sink costs should roughly track that tier's income band so progression doesn't stall or accelerate out of control
- **Inflation control**: track total DC in circulation (via `currency_ledger`) weekly; if it's trending up faster than active player count, that's a sign sinks are too weak relative to income and tuning/business formulas need adjusting
- **NC should never be a required sink** — model the DC-only progression curve as if NC didn't exist at all; NC spend should only ever affect cosmetic/convenience pacing, never the DC/Merit progression math
- **Merit sink design**: since Merit is achievement-earned and can't be bought, keep Merit-only items rare/prestige-flavored (unique cosmetics, titles) rather than pricing anything functionally necessary in Merit — otherwise achievement-light players get functionally blocked
- **Validate with the admin economy dashboard** (Section 12) — this model is a starting hypothesis, not gospel; revisit it once real player data exists

## 16. Quality Bar Checklist (self-check before considering any system "done")
This is not a new feature list — it's a rubric to check each system in Sections 1-9 against before moving to the next one in the Build Order:
- **Choice, not just numbers**: does this system make the player choose between real trade-offs (risk vs. safety, speed vs. safety, spend vs. save), or does it just let them grind a number upward with no decision involved?
- **Session-friendly**: can a player make meaningful progress in a 10-15 minute session, not just in multi-hour sessions?
- **Visible short + long-term goals**: does the player always have an obvious "what do I do right now" and an obvious "what am I working toward eventually"?
- **Fair losses**: when a player loses (busted, robbed, out-raced, out-hacked), do they understand why, and do they retain some agency (bail, escape roll, insurance, rematch) rather than just eating a loss with no recourse?
- **New players aren't prey**: does this system respect the level-tier attack protection (Section 10) and avoid creating a backdoor way to grief low-tier players?
- **Responsive & fast**: for anything with a minigame or timing element (hacking sequences, drift meter, race timing), has it been checked for input latency, not just correctness?
- **Failure teaches**: does a failed action tell the player *why* it failed (stress too high, firewall too strong) instead of just "you failed"?
- **Server-authoritative**: does every outcome-affecting check happen server-side, with the client only submitting an attempt (per Engineering Principles)?
- **Logged**: does every currency/resource change from this system write a ledger row (per Section 14's `currency_ledger` / equivalent)?
- **Reused, not rebuilt**: per Rule #0, was the existing implementation (if any) found, audited, and extended/fixed rather than replaced wholesale?
